CHAPTER I
THE PROJECT AND ITS BACKGROUND
Project Title
BUDGETBITE: Budget-Based Meal Recommendation and Scheduled Food Reservation System for Local Carinderias with Web and Mobile Integration
1.1 Project Background
Carinderias are an important part of Filipino communities because they provide affordable and accessible meals to students, employees, and other individuals. Many people rely on carinderias for their daily meals because of their reasonable prices and variety of food choices. However, customers sometimes experience difficulties finding meals that fit their available budget, especially when they have a limited amount of money to spend.
Another common problem is the waiting time customers experience when ordering food, particularly during busy hours. Customers may need to wait while meals are being prepared, and they may not always know whether their preferred food is still available. On the other hand, carinderia owners often manage orders, reservations, and food preparation manually, which can make it difficult to organize customer orders efficiently.
To address these concerns, the researchers propose BudgetBite, a budget-based meal recommendation and scheduled food reservation system with web and mobile integration. The system allows customers to enter their available budget and browse meals that match their spending limit. Customers can select their preferred meals, specify quantities, and schedule their expected arrival or pickup time.
The system also provides carinderia sellers with a web-based platform where they can manage food menus, prices, availability, reservations, and food preparation schedules. In addition, BudgetBite will include an online payment feature that allows customers to pay for their reservations using supported digital payment methods.
Through these features, BudgetBite aims to make meal selection, reservation, payment, and food preparation more convenient for customers and more organized for local carinderias.
1.2 Statement of the Problem
The project aims to address the following problems:
1.	Difficulty Finding Affordable Meals – Customers may have difficulty finding meals that match their available budget.
2.	Long Waiting Times – Customers often experience delays when ordering food, especially during peak hours.
3.	Lack of Advance Food Reservation – Many carinderias do not have a digital platform that allows customers to reserve meals before arriving.
4.	Inefficient Food Preparation Scheduling – Sellers may find it difficult to organize meal preparation based on customer arrival times and existing orders.
5.	Uncertainty of Food Availability – Customers may not know whether their preferred meals are still available before visiting the carinderia.
6.	Manual Order Management – Managing customer orders and reservations manually can result in confusion, missed orders, or scheduling difficulties.
7.	Limited Payment Options – Customers may prefer digital payment instead of paying in cash, but some carinderias may not have an organized online payment option for advance reservations.
1.3 Objectives of the Project
General Objective
The general objective of this project is to develop BudgetBite, a budget-based meal recommendation and scheduled food reservation system with web and mobile integration that allows customers to find affordable meals, reserve food in advance, select their expected arrival time, and pay online through supported payment methods. It also aims to help local carinderias manage their food menus, reservations, payments, and preparation schedules efficiently.
Specific Objectives
The project specifically aims to:
1.	Develop a mobile application that allows customers to register, log in, and manage their accounts.
2.	Provide a budget-based meal recommendation feature that displays available meals according to the customer's specified budget.
3.	Allow customers to view food details, including pictures, descriptions, prices, and availability.
4.	Enable customers to select meals, quantities, and preferred pickup or arrival times.
5.	Develop a reservation management feature that allows sellers to accept or reject customer reservations.
6.	Provide a suggested food preparation schedule based on the customer's expected arrival time and estimated preparation duration.
7.	Develop a web-based dashboard that allows carinderia sellers to manage menus, prices, food availability, reservations, and order statuses.
8.	Integrate an online payment feature that allows customers to pay for their reservations using supported digital payment methods.
9.	Provide payment verification, transaction records, and digital receipts for successful online payments.
10.	Provide an alternative cash-on-pickup payment option.
11.	Implement notifications that inform customers about reservation confirmations, payment updates, and order status changes.
12.	Evaluate the system based on functionality, usability, reliability, performance, and compatibility.
1.4 Scope and Limitations
Scope
The BudgetBite system will focus on budget-based meal recommendations, scheduled food reservations, food preparation coordination, and payment management for participating local carinderias.
The system will include the following features:
1. User Account Management
•	Customer registration and login.
•	Seller and administrator accounts.
•	User profile management.
•	Role-based access.
2. Budget-Based Meal Recommendation
•	Customers can enter their available budget.
•	The system displays meals that fit within the specified budget.
•	Customers can view meal pictures, descriptions, prices, and availability.
•	The system calculates the total cost based on selected meals and quantities.
3. Food Menu Management
•	Sellers can add, update, and remove food items.
•	Sellers can upload food pictures and descriptions.
•	Sellers can update food prices and availability.
4. Scheduled Food Reservation
•	Customers can select meals and quantities.
•	Customers can choose their expected arrival or pickup time.
•	Sellers can accept or reject reservations.
•	The system records reservation details and order status.
5. Food Preparation Scheduling
•	The system provides suggested preparation schedules based on estimated preparation time and customer arrival time.
•	Sellers can view upcoming reservations.
•	Sellers can update food preparation and order statuses.
6. Order Tracking and Notifications
•	Customers can monitor their reservation status.
•	The system sends notifications about reservation confirmation, payment updates, and food preparation progress.
•	Sellers can update customers when their orders are ready for pickup.
7. Online Payment
•	Customers can pay for accepted reservations through supported online payment methods.
•	The system will integrate with an online payment gateway, such as Gcash or another supported provider.
•	The system will display payment details and transaction status.
•	Successful payments will be recorded in the system.
•	Customers can receive digital receipts for completed online payments.
•	Customers can also choose cash-on-pickup as an alternative payment method.
8. Payment Management
•	Sellers can view the payment status of reservations.
•	The system will record payment transactions and payment history.
•	The system will distinguish between pending, successful, failed, and refunded payment statuses, where applicable.
9. Sales and Reservation Reports
•	Sellers can view reservation records and sales summaries.
•	Administrators can monitor registered users, participating carinderias, and system activities.
10. Web and Mobile Integration
•	Customers will access the system through a mobile application.
•	Sellers and administrators will access the system through a web-based platform.
•	Information will be synchronized through a centralized backend and database.
Limitations
The BudgetBite system will have the following limitations:
1.	The initial version will focus on food reservation and pickup. It will not include food delivery services.
2.	Customers and sellers must have an internet connection to access the system's online features.
3.	The accuracy of food menus, prices, and availability will depend on the information provided and updated by the sellers.
4.	The system will provide suggested food preparation schedules but cannot guarantee that food will always be served at an exact temperature or at the exact arrival time.
5.	Actual preparation and serving times may vary depending on kitchen workload, food availability, and unexpected delays.
6.	Online payments will depend on the availability and supported payment methods of the integrated payment gateway. Transaction fees, service interruptions, and payment processing delays may affect transactions.
7.	Cash-on-pickup will remain an alternative for customers who do not want to use online payment.
8.	The system will not provide professional nutritional advice or medical dietary recommendations.
9.	The initial version will focus on participating local carinderias and will not automatically include every food establishment.
10.	The system will not include advanced artificial intelligence-based recommendations or delivery tracking.

1.6 Project Description
BudgetBite is a web and mobile integrated system designed to connect customers with participating local carinderias. It provides a convenient way for customers to find meals based on their available budget, reserve their preferred food in advance, schedule their expected arrival time, and make payments.
The mobile application will serve as the main platform for customers. Through the application, users can browse available carinderias, enter their budget, view recommended meals, select food items, specify quantities, and reserve their orders. Customers can also choose between online payment and cash-on-pickup.
The web-based platform will be used by carinderia sellers to manage their food menus, prices, availability, customer reservations, payment records, and food preparation schedules. Once a reservation is accepted, the system will provide a suggested preparation schedule to help sellers organize orders according to customers' expected arrival times.
The system will also include an administrator dashboard for managing user accounts, participating carinderias, and system activities.
BudgetBite will consist of three main interfaces:
1.	Customer Mobile Application – Allows customers to browse meals, enter budgets, reserve food, select pickup times, make payments, and track orders.
2.	Seller Web Dashboard – Allows carinderia sellers to manage menus, reservations, food preparation schedules, payment records, and sales.
3.	Administrator Dashboard – Allows administrators to manage accounts, monitor carinderias, and oversee system activities.
An online payment gateway will be integrated to process digital payments securely. The system will record payment statuses and transaction details to help customers and sellers monitor their transactions.
1.7 Proposed Solution
To address the identified problems, the researchers propose BudgetBite as a centralized web and mobile system for budget-based meal recommendations and scheduled food reservations.
The proposed system will provide the following solutions:
1.	Budget-Based Meal Filtering – Customers can enter their available budget and view meals that match their spending limit.
2.	Advance Food Reservation – Customers can reserve meals before arriving at the carinderia, reducing the need to order only after reaching the establishment.
3.	Scheduled Food Preparation – The system provides sellers with suggested preparation schedules based on customer arrival times and estimated preparation durations.
4.	Food Availability Management – Sellers can update food availability so customers can view currently available meals.
5.	Online Payment Integration – Customers can pay for accepted reservations through supported online payment methods.
6.	Cash-on-Pickup Option – Customers who prefer paying in person can choose cash-on-pickup.
7.	Payment Verification and Records – The system records payment statuses and transaction details to help prevent confusion regarding paid and unpaid reservations.
8.	Order Status Notifications – Customers receive updates about reservation confirmation, payment status, food preparation, and pickup readiness.
9.	Centralized Reservation Management – Sellers can manage incoming reservations and monitor order progress through the web dashboard.
10.	Web and Mobile Synchronization – Customer reservations and seller updates will be synchronized through a centralized database.
Through these features, BudgetBite aims to improve the convenience of meal selection and reservation for customers while helping local carinderias organize their daily operations.

1.9 Non-Functional Requirements
The BudgetBite system must meet the following non-functional requirements:
1.	Performance – The system should respond efficiently when users browse meals, submit reservations, and access information.
2.	Security – The system must protect user accounts, personal information, and transaction records through secure authentication and appropriate data protection.
3.	Usability – The system should provide a simple, understandable, and user-friendly interface for customers, sellers, and administrators.
4.	Reliability – The system should process reservations and record transactions accurately.
5.	Compatibility – The mobile application should support compatible mobile devices, while the web platform should work with modern web browsers.
6.	Maintainability – The system should be designed so that future updates and improvements can be implemented easily.
7.	Scalability – The system should be capable of accommodating additional users and participating carinderias as it develops.
8.	Availability – The system should be accessible whenever its hosting services and internet connection are available.
9.	Data Integrity – The system should maintain accurate and consistent information about menus, reservations, orders, and payments.
10.	Synchronization – Changes made through the web platform should be reflected in the mobile application and vice versa.
11.	Payment Security – Online payment processing must use the integrated payment gateway's secure procedures. Payment confirmation must be verified through the backend rather than relying only on the customer's return to the application.
1.10 System Workflow
The BudgetBite system will follow the workflow below:
Customer Workflow
1.	The customer opens the BudgetBite mobile application.
2.	The customer registers or logs in.
3.	The customer selects a participating carinderia.
4.	The customer enters their available budget.
5.	The system displays meals that match the specified budget.
6.	The customer selects preferred meals and quantities.
7.	The system calculates the total cost and remaining budget.
8.	The customer selects an expected arrival or pickup time.
9.	The customer reviews and submits the reservation.
10.	The seller receives the reservation request.
11.	The seller reviews and accepts or rejects the reservation.
12.	If rejected, the customer receives a notification and may submit another reservation.
13.	If accepted, the customer proceeds to payment selection.
14.	The customer chooses one of the following payment methods:
o	Online Payment: The customer completes payment through the integrated payment gateway using an available supported method.
o	Cash-on-Pickup: The customer chooses to pay in person when collecting the order.
15.	For online payments, the system verifies the transaction through the backend and records the payment status.
16.	If online payment fails, the customer may retry or select another available payment method, subject to the reservation's payment rules.
17.	Once the reservation is confirmed and the required payment condition is satisfied, the seller receives the order for preparation.
18.	The system provides a suggested food preparation schedule based on the expected arrival time.
19.	The seller prepares the reserved meals.
20.	The seller updates the order status.
21.	The customer receives a notification when the order is ready.
22.	The customer arrives at the carinderia.
23.	The seller verifies the reservation using its order number or QR code.
24.	The seller releases the prepared order.
25.	For cash-on-pickup reservations, the customer pays at the counter.
26.	The system updates the reservation and payment records.
27.	The order is marked as completed.
Seller Workflow
1.	The seller logs in to the web dashboard.
2.	The seller manages food menus, prices, and availability.
3.	The seller receives incoming customer reservations.
4.	The seller reviews reservation details, including meals, quantities, arrival time, and payment information.
5.	The seller accepts or rejects the reservation.
6.	The seller views the suggested food preparation schedule for accepted reservations.
7.	The seller prepares the reserved meals.
8.	The seller updates the order status.
9.	The seller verifies the customer's reservation upon pickup.
10.	The seller confirms cash collection when the customer selected cash-on-pickup.
11.	The system updates the order and payment records.
12.	The seller can view reservation history and sales reports.
Administrator Workflow
1.	The administrator logs in to the administrator dashboard.
2.	The administrator manages customer and seller accounts.
3.	The administrator verifies and manages participating carinderias.
4.	The administrator monitors reservations and system activities.
5.	The administrator reviews relevant transaction and system reports.
Overall System Process
