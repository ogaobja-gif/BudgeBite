# BUDGETBITE
# Budget-Based Meal Recommendation and Scheduled Food Reservation System for Local Carinderias with Web and Mobile Integration

# Project Overview

'BudgetBite' is a web and mobile integrated system designed to connect customers with participating local carinderias. It allows customers to find affordable meals based on their available budget, reserve food in advance, select their expected arrival or pickup time, and pay through supported payment methods.

The system also provides carinderia sellers with a web-based dashboard to manage food menus, prices, availability, reservations, payment records, and food preparation schedules.

BudgetBite aims to make meal selection and food reservations more convenient for customers while helping local carinderias organize their daily operations efficiently.

# Project Objectives

# General Objective

To develop a budget-based meal recommendation and scheduled food reservation system with web and mobile integration that allows customers to find affordable meals, reserve food in advance, select their expected arrival time, and pay online through supported payment methods. It also aims to help local carinderias manage their food menus, reservations, payments, and preparation schedules efficiently.

# Specific Objectives

1. Develop a mobile application for customer registration, login, and account management.
2. Provide budget-based meal recommendations.
3. Allow customers to view food pictures, descriptions, prices, and availability.
4. Enable customers to select meals, quantities, and preferred pickup times.
5. Provide reservation management for sellers.
6. Generate suggested food preparation schedules.
7. Develop a web-based seller dashboard.
8. Integrate supported online payment methods.
9. Provide payment verification, transaction records, and digital receipts.
10. Support cash-on-pickup payments.
11. Implement notifications for reservation and order updates.
12. Evaluate the system based on functionality, usability, reliability, performance, and compatibility.

# Main System Features

- User Account Management – Customer, seller, and administrator accounts.
- Budget-Based Meal Recommendation – Displays meals according to the customer's available budget.
- Food Menu Management – Sellers can add, update, and remove food items.
- Food Availability Management – Sellers can update available meals.
- Scheduled Food Reservation – Customers can reserve meals and select pickup times.
- Food Preparation Scheduling – Provides suggested preparation schedules based on expected arrival times.
- Order Tracking and Notifications – Customers can monitor their reservation progress.
- Online Payment Integration– Supports available digital payment methods.
- Cash-on-Pickup – Allows customers to pay when collecting their orders.
- Sales and Reservation Reports – Helps sellers monitor transactions and reservations.

# Project Scope and Limitations

BudgetBite focuses on budget-based meal recommendations, scheduled food reservations, food preparation coordination, and payment management for participating local carinderias.

#Limitations

- The initial version focuses on food reservation and pickup, not delivery.
- An internet connection is required for online features.
- Food availability and pricing depend on seller updates.
- Preparation schedules are suggestions and cannot guarantee exact serving times.
- Online payment availability depends on the integrated payment provider.
- The system does not provide professional nutritional or medical dietary advice.
- Advanced AI-based recommendations and delivery tracking are not included.
